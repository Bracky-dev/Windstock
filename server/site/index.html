Bracky

Pokémon GO, like it's 2016 again. A private, offline world made just for you — no accounts, no ads, no remote servers. You sign in from the game itself.

Gameplay

Logging in

Tap "Pokémon Trainer Club" and use any username you prefer. The login doesn't require a password and doesn't create a new account; it only saves your trainer name.

Catching

Wild Pokémon spawn depending on the real-world terrain in your vicinity — Water Pokémon near bodies of water, Bug and Grass Pokémon in forests, and some Pokémon only after dark. Tap the Pokémon, throw a Poké Ball, and it's yours.

PokéStops & Gyms

Spin the PokéStops around you to gather Poké Balls, Berries, Potions, and other items. Gyms are used for battling and representing your team there.

Nests

Some areas are called "nests", meaning Pokémon of one kind will spawn frequently. They change every few weeks — it's always a good idea to revisit places you like.

Eggs

You can hatch an egg by dropping it in an incubator and walking some distance.

Shop

You earn PokéCoins that can be spent in the Shop on Poké Balls, Lures, Incense, Lucky Eggs, Incubators, and bag capacity increases.

House rules

  • Be nice

    This is a world we share together — make sure to treat other players accordingly.

  • Whatever happens in Bracky stays in Bracky

    No drama, just fun.

  • Your progress follows you

    Progress is saved to the server, so you can switch devices whenever you want and resume from where you left off.

  • Something broke?

    It's most likely a problem on my side, not yours — report it and I'll fix it.

Fine print (very little of it)

No Niantic, no purchases with real money, and no data collection. Bracky is my passion project that allows me to revive the good old times.

Happy catching — go catch 'em all!
